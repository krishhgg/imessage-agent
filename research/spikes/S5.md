# S5: fixtures and token benchmark

Status: done. Fixture done. Benchmark corrected through two fix-and-review rounds (`bench/VERIFY.md`); `bench/results.md` is current.

## Fixture

`Tests/Fixtures/build.sh` generates `Tests/Fixtures/chat.db` from `research/chatdb-schema.sql` (the real macOS 26.2 schema). It passes 7,348 checks and builds byte-identically.
- 1,534 messages in 8 chats: self-chat, 1:1 iMessage with SMS fallback, a named group, an unnamed group, an email handle, RCS and SMS.
- 93% of bodies are attributedBody only. All 1,526 decode with NSUnarchiver.
- It covers every tapback type, stickers, group events, unread rows, attachments (including 2 offloaded to iCloud), deletes and Recently Deleted.
- The self-chat echo row shape is a guess. S2 checks it against real rows.

## First benchmark result and its review

The first run reported ours as 1.1x to 17.7x lighter on T1 to T3. The independent check (`bench/VERIFY.md`) found that three competitors were asked for many more messages than ours for the same task. With equal limits, the range is about 1.1x to 5.7x, and no tool comes out 10x heavier on tokens alone. The 650-token placeholder skill made up 62% of our T1 to T3 tokens.

The leanest competitors (cmer at 1.1x and RyanNewsom at 1.3x) are close on tokens. They cover fewer tasks: neither does unread state, tapbacks or group file sends.

## Design changes it caused

- `chats` shows each chat's newest message with time and sender, so "react to the last message" needs no extra read.
- New `unread [-c 2]` verb, so "summarize unread" is one call.
- `send` text is optional with `--file`.
- The skill body budget drops from 650 to 400 tokens.

## Corrected benchmark (two review rounds)

Every tool with a history limit gets the same limit, and ours is a renderer of the DESIGN.md section 5 CLI with the 445-token `skill/imessage/SKILL.md`.

| Measure, T1 to T3 | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Input tokens, others vs ours | 1.5x (cmer) to 7.7x (jeffhuber) |
| Steps, others vs ours | 1.0x (RyanNewsom) to 2.8x (jeffhuber) |
| With a 628-token skill | 1.2x to 6.2x |
| With a skill as large as imsg's (1,906 tokens) | 0.5x to 2.7x. cmer and RyanNewsom beat ours |
| Task coverage | ours 2 yes plus 3 unverified, pending S1 to S3. imsg and steve228uk also attempt all five tasks, with their sends unverified because osascript from the Grok Bot shell may get -1743. Everyone else has "no" on T4 and T5 |

What this means:
- Tokens alone won't give 10x against the lean tools. The skill size is still the deciding factor, so it has to stay small.
- The advantage that holds up is coverage plus cost. Ours is the only design that does all five tasks from Grok Bot without the -1743 risk, if S1 to S3 pass. It also stays cheap on tokens and steps.
