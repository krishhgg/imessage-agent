# Research notes

These notes record what was read, measured and decided while designing iMessage Agent. Each claim is tagged verified, reported, inferred or measured.

Citations to paths under `/tmp/` (for example `/tmp/gbim-research/...` or `/tmp/s5-...`) point at scratch clones and scripts that existed only on the machine where the research ran. They aren't in this repo. The upstream projects are listed with links in [repos.md](repos.md), so you can check a claim against the source.

`spikes/` holds the spike results measured on one Mac (macOS 26.2, Messages 14.0), and `live-tests/` holds the live results from the bridge itself.
